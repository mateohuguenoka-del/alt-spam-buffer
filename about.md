# Alt-Spam Input Buffer

Makes alternate spamming reliable.

- **Input buffer**: a click made in mid-air is remembered (default 60 ms) and replayed on the exact step you land.
- **Alt-spam safe**: releasing one key while another is held no longer sends a stray release; overlapping presses become fresh clicks.
- **Ghost players**: a translucent icon shows where a buffered click was queued.

All options are in the mod settings.
